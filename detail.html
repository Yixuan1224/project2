The Language of Flowers

Floral fragrances are more than just the smell of flowers. From fresh and delicate to rich and romantic, different floral notes can create different moods and impressions.

01 — Rose

Rose is one of the most recognizable floral notes in perfumery. It can smell fresh and dewy, soft and powdery, or rich and sweet.

02 — Jasmine

Jasmine is known for its rich, sweet, and expressive scent. It often adds warmth and depth to floral fragrances.

03 — Peony

Peony is often represented as a light, fresh, and airy floral accord. It brings a soft and delicate feeling to modern perfumes.

FAQ

What are floral notes?

Floral notes are scents that resemble flowers, such as rose, jasmine, and peony.

Do all floral scents smell the same?

No. Some floral scents are fresh and light, while others are rich and sweet.
